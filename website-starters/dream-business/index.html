Before the first sketch

Every good project
starts with a question.

Use this small checklist to put your idea into words. A notebook is all you need; nothing is submitted or collected here.

  1. Who are you making this for?

    Picture one person and the thing they are trying to accomplish.

  2. What needs to become clearer?

    Name the one problem the project should help that person solve.

  3. What would a useful first version do?

    Choose a small outcome you could show, try, and improve.
