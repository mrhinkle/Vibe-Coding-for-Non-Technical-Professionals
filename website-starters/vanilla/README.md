# Vanilla

A clean white page with plain typography. Open `index.html` directly in your browser. It works offline, without installation.

Copy this whole folder into your own website project before editing. Give your chatbot the actual `index.html`, not just its filename.

1. Change the headline and introductory paragraph.
2. Change two colors in the style section, then check text contrast.
3. Change one section to reflect your idea.

**Check:** the “See the next step” link moves to its matching section. Try the navigation, keyboard focus, and a narrow window. Keep the fan-site attribution if retaining All Things AI branding. No form submissions, bookings, purchases, or signups happen on this page.
