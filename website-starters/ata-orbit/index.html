Why I’m a fan

The best part is what
you do with it.

01

Find your spark.

Take an ordinary task and ask an unusual question. Curiosity is a perfectly good place to begin.

02

Make it tangible.

A tiny website. A silly game. One useful tool. Something you can open beats an idea you keep postponing.

03

Stay human.

Bring your taste, judgment, and sense of humor. Check what the machine gives you. Keep the good bits.
