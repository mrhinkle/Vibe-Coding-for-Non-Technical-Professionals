A solo game. Choose an empty square to start a battle for it. Win the battle and the square is yours (). Lose it and the square goes to the (). The never takes a turn on its own.

Three of your marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins. Three marks loses. A full board with no line is a draw.

The battle

Each side has 2 health. The shows a move; you pick a response. A correct response takes 1 health from the . A wrong one takes 1 health from you. The first side to reach 0 loses the battle.
