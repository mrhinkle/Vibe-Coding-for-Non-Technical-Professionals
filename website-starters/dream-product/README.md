# Daylight desk lamp starter

A fictional product concept with a large original lamp illustration, three design qualities, and three suggested spaces. Everything is in `index.html`; it works offline without installation, external images, fonts, or scripts.

## Open it

Open `index.html` in your browser. The main action, **Choose your space**, takes you to three suggested settings for the lamp. These are inspiration, not purchase options. No checkout, price, order, or customer-data collection is included.

## Three customization tasks

1. Rename the product and rewrite its one-sentence promise. Keep claims clearly about a fictional concept unless you have verified facts for a real product.
2. Replace the three product qualities and the three suggested spaces. Keep `id="spaces"` on the destination section.
3. Adjust the CSS color variables. The illustration uses its own matching SVG colors; ask your chatbot to update those consistently if you change the palette.

Find `CUSTOMIZE` comments to begin. Keep a working copy, reopen the actual edited file, and check the main action, all navigation links, keyboard focus, and a narrow layout. The sample SVG is editable original artwork; it is not a photograph of a manufactured product.
